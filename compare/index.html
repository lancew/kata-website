How these comparisons are made

Claims about other tools come from their own published sources — their websites and repositories — and are reviewed periodically (last reviewed September 2026). Where a feature could not be confirmed from a project’s own material we mark it “not evidenced” rather than claiming it is missing. We make Judo Kata Tournament Manager, so check the sources yourself before deciding.
