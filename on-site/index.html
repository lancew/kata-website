Have us run the scoring at your next kata competition

We travel to your competition and run the system on the day — in the UK or internationally — so judges and clerks can focus on the mat, not on setup or spreadsheets.

Check availability for your event

Run by a member of the IJF and EJU IT teams.

What’s included

  • The system set up and tested before your first performance
  • An operator at the laptop for the whole day, with mat-clerk oversight
  • Judge tablets seated and configured for your panel
  • Live results and the announcer board on your hall screens
  • Printable PDF score packs for the table
  • Results and technique-level CSV data handed over at the end
  • Help planning the hardware you need, or bring kit and we’ll run it

The software itself is described on the home page and how an event runs.

Low-risk by default

Not ready to switch? Run in parallel with your existing scoring process at a real event — your spreadsheet stays the safety net until you’re confident. A smaller event is an ideal first run before scaling up to a larger competition.

How it works

  1. Email your event date, kata, and rough number of pairs.
  2. We agree the scope, kit, and a fixed fee.
  3. We arrive early, set up, and test before the first pair.
  4. You run your event; we hand over the results and data afterwards.

Typical fee

£100 per day (including travel days), plus accommodation, meals, travel costs, and any hardware agreed in advance.

Prefer to run it without us there?

We can post you a pre-configured Raspberry Pi with the whole system installed. Plug it into your venue network and it’s ready to run — all it needs is a static IP address. You get the full set-up without the travel cost.

Or we can bring the hardware too

We can bring the whole set-up to your venue — judge tablets, projector(s), the server, and network kit — configure it, and run the event. Priced on your requirements, so tell us your kata, pair count, and screens and we’ll quote.
