# Incumbent: Kata Scoresheets (EJU) — Michel Kozlowski

**Last verified:** 2026-09-30
**Source:** https://sites.google.com/site/katascoresheets/ (linked from this site's scoring page; may require sign-in)

## Identity
- **Name:** Kata Scoresheets (EJU)
- **Author:** Michel Kozlowski (d. 2023) — this project is dedicated to his memory
- **Type:** Excel spreadsheet (EJU-aligned scoring)
- **Pricing:** free

## Positioning
The reference EJU kata scoresheet many organisers still use. It is the incumbent
this product is measured against, and the basis for the EJU ruleset here.

## Feature notes
- **Scoring:** EJU ruleset, computed in a spreadsheet
- **Devices:** none — one operator, one file
- **Live display:** none
- **Manual totals:** entered by hand at the table
- **Audit trail:** spreadsheets and versions, not a per-tap log
- **Data:** shared via files/copies rather than a single live event database

## Strengths (be fair)
- Familiar and trusted; many organisers already know it
- Free and proven over years
- EJU-aligned

## Weaknesses
- Single-user file: no live judge devices and nothing for the hall
- Totals are entered by hand — slow and error-prone under time pressure
- No per-tap audit trail; corrections are manual
- All screens and paperwork are separate manual steps

## Best for
- Organisers who tally on a laptop/spreadsheet and are happy with that process

## Not ideal for
- Events wanting judge tablets, live hall results, and automated packs

## Relationship
- This product implements the EJU ruleset and follows Kozlowski's sheets; it is
  explicitly dedicated to his memory. Frame any comparison respectfully.
